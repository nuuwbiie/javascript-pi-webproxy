# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

React, Vite, and strict TypeScript. The experience is a static frontend, uses npm, and must remain deployable to Vercel without a backend.

## Users

- Primary: Pekan Ilkomerz 62 peers, mentors, and assignment reviewers exploring the team.
- Secondary: friends and future collaborators viewing the project as a digital yearbook and portfolio artifact.
- Maintainers: student team members replacing profile, CV, social, and activity content without changing application logic.

## Product Purpose

PROXY OS presents a university group and its Pekan Ilkomerz memories through an interactive fictional operating system. Success means visitors can quickly meet every member, inspect published CVs, understand the group, and explore activity documentation on desktop or mobile.

## Positioning

A digital yearbook disguised as a playful operating system: profiles, documents, and memories are discovered as apps and files rather than read as a conventional portfolio page.

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive from a shared link, experience a short session-scoped boot, then explore a composed desktop or a purpose-built mobile home screen. No sign-in, uploads, server data, or autoplay media are required.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Desktop window manager with open, close, focus, stacking, minimize, maximize, restore, drag, and resize.
- Mobile applications open as full-screen views; dragging and desktop chrome are removed.
- Members, profiles, CV explorer/viewer, memories gallery/lightbox, About, and Links.
- Keyboard operability, reduced-motion support, responsive layouts, and graceful missing-content states.
- Personal information may only be published when explicitly supplied. Current missing names, profiles, social links, portraits, CV PDFs, and activity photos remain clearly marked sample placeholders.
- GitHub-ready and Vercel-ready static build.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: PROXY OS.
- Assignment: Connect & Deploy — Pekan Ilkomerz 62.
- Voice: friendly, concise, human, and lightly playful.
- Binding visual reference: `C:/Users/ibnur/Downloads/a_wide_clean_colorful_ui_concept_art_collage_s.png`.
- Avoid Windows/macOS imitation, cyberpunk styling, excessive glass, and generic SaaS presentation.

## Evidence on Hand

- One supplied concept-art collage showing boot, welcome, desktop, member explorer, profile, CV viewer, and gallery states.
- No verified member records, social URLs, portrait photos, CV PDFs, repository URL, or Pekan Ilkomerz activity photographs were provided. Future work must not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

1. The OS metaphor serves discovery instead of obstructing it.
2. Real people and memories stay central; chrome remains supporting material.
3. Desktop and mobile share content but use device-appropriate interaction models.
4. Missing content fails honestly and usefully.
5. Every playful interaction remains keyboard-accessible and motion-conscious.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA principles: semantic controls, visible focus, keyboard navigation, usable touch targets, sufficient contrast, descriptive labels, and a reduced-motion path that bypasses decorative timing.
